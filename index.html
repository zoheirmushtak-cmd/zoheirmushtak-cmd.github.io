Unconventional Wisdom Uploader

Unconventional Wisdom Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Unconventional Wisdom YouTube channel to manage that channel's own videos.

It uploads videos and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, captions and thumbnails through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner uses it.
